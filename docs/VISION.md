# Vision — Tablework (working name; transcriptor today)

## Why this exists

A cast learns a play together but line-learning apps are built around one
actor and one phone. A few now share a script to a cast (ADR 001, amended
2026-10-01), and none of them share the company's VOICES: each actor still
hears a different reading, keeps their own notes, and nobody knows who is
off book. The stage manager's cues live in a separate binder. This app is
the rehearsal room: one script, one set of voices, one sound design,
everyone's place and progress in it.

## Who it is for

- Primary: a small theatre company in rehearsal. The director or stage
  manager sets up the production and invites the cast.
- Secondary: an actor rehearsing alone. A production of one, same tool.
- Not for: self-tape and audition workflows; acting classes; script writing.

## North star

Casts that go off book in this app. Metric: productions where every cast
member has a clean run of their whole part.

## What done looks like

A director drops a PDF on Claude, says "load this into Tablework", invites
the cast by link, and by first rehearsal every actor has heard the play in
the company's voices and started drilling their lines. In the room, the
stage manager runs cues from it, everyone follows the leader's place, and the
director sees who is off book and where each actor is weak.

## What it is NOT

- Not another AI scene partner for solo actors. That market has ten of them.
- Not a self-tape tool. No camera.
- Not a script marketplace. Users bring scripts they have the right to use;
  a script is private to its production.
- Not an iOS app store product. Signup and billing are on the web only.

## Principles specific to this product

- The production is the unit: ownership, billing, sharing, voices and cues
  hang off it. A solo actor is a production of one.
- Voices cost real money; the price of a render is shown before it is spent
  and a rendered line is never rendered twice for anyone.
- Everything works without an account for the first five minutes (paste a
  script, run lines with browser voices). Accounts are for keeping and
  sharing, not for trying.
