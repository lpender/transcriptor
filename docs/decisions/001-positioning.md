# 001 — A rehearsal-room tool for a company, not a solo line runner

- Date: 2026-09-30
- Status: Accepted

## Context

Productionizing transcriptor means competing. The solo line-learning space is
full: Offbook, coldRead, ActOnCue, blablabla, Acting Pal, plus older
record-and-play tools. All are one actor, one phone. None serve a whole cast,
none run the show's sound, none ingest by MCP. Transcriptor already has
leader/follower, per-role stats, shared notes and scene cues.

## Decision

Position as the rehearsal room for a production: director loads the script
once, invites the cast, everyone runs lines against the same voices, the stage
manager runs cues, the ensemble follows the leader in the room. Solo rehearsal
is included, not the pitch.

## Alternatives rejected

- Fifth solo AI scene partner — commodity, priced at $12/month by others,
  differentiated only by price.
- Stage-management tool only (cues, calls) — no daily use; line learning is
  what brings people back.

## Consequences

- Ownership, sharing and billing are per production (see 003).
- The name should say company, rehearsal or table, not "off book" (four
  products already use it).
- The show-specific sound design becomes an upload feature per production.

## Amendment, 2026-10-01 — "all are one actor, one phone" is no longer true

Checked against the live products. Three now sell to a company, so the gap the
positioning claimed has narrowed:

- **ActOnCue Stage** — a director uploads the script and shares one link; the
  cast learn with no account each, with chosen voices, a progressive
  hide-the-text mode and a cue-only audio mode. Priced by use: $0.35 per
  rehearsal hour per actor ("10 actors x 2h each x $0.35/hr = $7.00"), free to
  start, education discounts.
- **Off Book! (LineSync)** — a director shares the show's lines and their own
  recordings to the whole cast by email invite, free for the invited actors;
  cast recordings appear back with the director.
- **OnBook Live** — a live shared prompt book for the stage management side:
  real-time speech recognition follows the dialogue so everyone's page tracks
  the room. No line learning or off-book standing seen.
- **coldRead** is $6.99–$10.99 a month, not the $12 recorded above;
  LineLearner $3.99 and Rehearsal Pro $19.99 remain one-off purchases.

What is still ours, and what the product must keep being judged on:

1. One set of voices per CHARACTER for the whole company, rendered once and
   shared, not each actor's own recordings or each actor's own voice picks.
2. The show's sound design — music and room tone per scene, cues pressed
   through like lines — in the same tool as the lines.
3. The director's off-book standing per actor (best clean run, the weak
   sentences, how long they have been quiet), which none of the three shows.
4. Everyone in the room on one place, led from one device.
5. Ingest and administration by MCP, so a director's AI loads the play.

The decision stands, but "nobody serves a cast" must not be said again — the
claim is "nobody serves a cast's voices, sound and standing in one room". The
pricing decision (003) is unaffected: $1 a seat a month is below every
subscription found, and a company rehearsing steadily beats ActOnCue's hourly
rate within the first month.
