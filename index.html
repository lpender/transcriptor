Tablework

Learn your lines with your whole cast.

One script, one set of voices, the stage manager's cues, and the director sees who is off book.

How it works · free to try, no account

Actors

Run your lines against the company's voices, alone or in the room. Misses are remembered and drilled. Works on your own for free.

Directors

Load the script once, invite the cast with one link, and see who is off book and where each actor is weak. An actor joins with their email and a link — no password to make up.

Stage managers

Music and room tone per scene, cues pressed through like lines, and everyone follows the leader's place.

A production is $1 a seat a month, or $10 a year, with 14 days free. Rendered voices are priced before you pay; the device's own voices cost nothing.

Paste your script

Paste a script — one speech per line, each starting with the character's name and a colon. Your place, your parts and your score are kept in this browser.
